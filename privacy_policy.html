Saju365 – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

🔒 Saju365 does not collect any personally identifiable information.
All birth data, profiles, and settings are stored locally on your device only.

1 Overview

Saju365 ("the App") is a Korean Four Pillars of Destiny (Saju/BaZi) reading app developed by GuardPhrase Studio. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, how it is stored, and your rights as a user.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2 Information We Collect

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers.

Data Storage Location Purpose
Birth date, time, and gender (entered by user) On-device (DataStore) Calculate and display Saju chart and fortune readings
Saved chart profiles (up to 10) On-device (DataStore) Allow users to save and reload multiple charts
Language preference (Korean / English) On-device (DataStore) Display app content in selected language
AI reading results (cached per session) On-device (DataStore) Avoid duplicate API calls; restore previous readings
Advertising identifier (ADID / GAID) Google AdMob servers (external) Serve banner and rewarded ads
🔒 Not transmitted to developers: Birth data, profiles, and settings remain on your device. Only advertising identifiers are shared with Google AdMob for ad delivery.

3 Purpose of Data Use

  • Calculate and display your Four Pillars of Destiny (Saju) chart
  • Provide daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, and lifetime fortune readings
  • Provide compatibility (Gunghap) readings between two charts
  • Find best and worst compatibility matches from calculated candidates
  • Restore previously generated AI readings without re-calling the API
  • Display banner and rewarded advertisements

4 AI Reading Service (OpenAI)

The App offers an optional AI-powered fortune reading feature using the OpenAI API (GPT-4o mini). When you tap "Get AI Reading," the following non-personal Saju chart data is sent to OpenAI:

  • Day Master (Heavenly Stem and Element)
  • Four Pillars chart structure (year, month, day, hour pillars)
  • Element balance, strength level, and favorable element
  • Personality trait tags and fortune scores
Note: No name, contact information, or device identifiers are sent to OpenAI. Only anonymous chart data is transmitted. Please refer to OpenAI's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle API data.

5 Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

Service Purpose Privacy Policy
Google AdMob Banner and rewarded advertisements Google Privacy Policy
OpenAI API AI-powered fortune readings (optional) OpenAI Privacy Policy

6 Data Retention and Deletion

  • All data is stored locally on your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the App
  • You can delete saved profiles at any time from the Profiles screen
  • AI reading cache is automatically reset at midnight each day (daily/weekly/monthly readings)
  • Lifetime readings are cached permanently until the App is uninstalled
  • The developer has no access to data stored on your device

7 App Permissions

  • Required Internet access — Required for AI readings, content updates, and ad delivery

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or device storage.

8 Your Rights

  • You can view, edit, or delete your saved profiles at any time within the App
  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID through your device settings
  • Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored data
  • For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at the address below

9 Children's Privacy

Saju365 is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used this App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10 Disclaimer

The fortune readings and compatibility analyses provided by Saju365 are for entertainment and personal reflection purposes only. They do not constitute professional advice of any kind. The App makes no guarantees regarding the accuracy or outcomes of any reading.
